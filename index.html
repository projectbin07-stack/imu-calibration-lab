IMU Calibration Lab

Calibrate an accelerometer, gyroscope or magnetometer from a logged CSV and measure its noise with Allan deviation. Your file is read in this browser and is never uploaded.

            Noise terms

            Error bars and +/- figures: 1/sqrt(2 (N/m - 1)) for a single point with N/m clusters. Points with fewer than about three independent clusters are drawn but not used for the read-offs.

            Export

            All bundled samples are synthetic: generated with known errors so the recovered values can be checked. Nothing here has been validated on a physical sensor yet. Methods and sources: docs/methods.md. MIT licence.
